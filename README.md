**Parish Administration Digitalized.**

Domus is a platform that helps parishes run their administration in one place: parishioner accounts, parish organizations, events and more. It is built for parish staff and volunteers, and aims to replace scattered spreadsheets and chat groups with a single, simple tool.

## Features

- **Accounts & Sign-in:** passwordless login via email, WhatsApp or Telegram one-time codes, or Google and GitHub. A guided onboarding links each person's WhatsApp and Telegram. Users can sign in from several devices.
- **Parish & Diocese Data:** multiple parishes in one system, with territorial structure (zones and wards), shared lookup lists and calendar periods.
- **Organizations:** parish councils, sections, categorical groups and wards/neighborhoods, with members, positions and time-limited invite links.
- **Events & RSVP:** organizations publish events that are either open to the whole parish or private to their members, and members can RSVP.
- **AI Assistant:** a chat assistant on the web, Telegram and WhatsApp that helps users with their parish data, within their own permissions.
